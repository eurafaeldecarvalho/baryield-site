BarYield

Linear cut optimizer for bars, tubes, profiles, rebar, pipe, conduit and lumber.

Enter the pieces you need and the stock bars you have. BarYield calculates a 1D cutting plan with the least waste, draws each bar, keeps everything on the phone, and can export a workshop PDF or CSV.

  • Kerf (saw thickness), end trims and a minimum reusable offcut.
  • Metric and imperial units, including simplified fractional inches (for example 3/4, not 12/16).
  • Works offline. No account or login.
  • Backup file so you can move projects to another phone.
  • Free for on-screen plans, with up to 3 saved projects and 1 material per project. Pro unlocks unlimited projects and materials, PDF/CSV export, offcut inventory, guided cutting, and a workshop name and logo on the PDF. There are no ads.

Em português. O BarYield é um otimizador de corte linear (1D). Você cadastra as peças e as barras em estoque; o app monta o plano com o menor desperdício, desenha cada barra e guarda tudo no aparelho. Funciona offline, sem conta. O Pro é vendido pelo Google Play.
